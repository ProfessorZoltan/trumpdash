PRIVACY POLICY

Trump Dash, a parody rhythm platformer. Effective September 3, 2026.

The short version

Trump Dash has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress and settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device only: best percentage and number of clears per level for regular and practice mode, and the practice, sound and sync-offset settings. Nothing in that storage leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Visit counting

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It records page views with the referring page, country, browser type and page performance timings. It does not use cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint devices and does not track you across other sites. Cloudflare processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The game is served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive your IP address and browser details with each request and may keep short-lived logs for security and operations, under Vercel's privacy policy.

Installed and Android versions

If you add the game to your home screen or install it from an app store, you get the same site inside an app shell. It requests no device permissions and collects nothing beyond what is described above. The app may cache the game's files on your device so it can start without a connection.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
